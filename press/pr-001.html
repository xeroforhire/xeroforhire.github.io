Xeroforhire.com Launches as Official Archive

OMAHA, ARK. — June 10, 2026 — Independent creator Jashae Slaughter has launched xeroforhire.com as the official home and archive for his creative work — a single, owned home for the writing, the podcast, the music, and the Apocalyptiverse, consolidating more than 14 years of work in one permanent, platform-independent place.

The site serves as the official archive and source of truth for the creative work of Jashae Slaughter, known online as Xeroforhire. It gathers his writing, comics, podcasting, and music under one roof, organized so that a first-time visitor can see at a glance that these are not disconnected projects but chapters in a single ongoing body of work.

After years of work distributed across third-party platforms, the site establishes a home base that does not depend on any single service's algorithm or longevity. "If a platform changes its terms or disappears, the complete picture of the work should remain intact and under the creator's control," is the principle behind the move.

The archive is organized by medium — Writing, Podcasting, and Music — alongside a provenance timeline, a media FAQ, and this press hub, which will publish official announcements on a regular cadence going forward. Readers can find the fiction and comics through Gumroad, Amazon, and GlobalComix, the podcast on Substack, and music as The Legend of XERO on Bandcamp and Apple Music.

About Jashae Slaughter

Jashae Slaughter, known online as Xeroforhire for more than 14 years, is a writer, podcaster, musician, and independent creator. He is the founder of the Apocalyptiverse press and creator of the sci-fi action thriller The Last Day, hosts The Xero for Hire Podcast, and records music as The Legend of XERO.
