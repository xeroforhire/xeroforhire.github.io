Apocalyptiverse.com Launches as an Interactive Fiction Platform

OMAHA, ARK. — December 12, 2025 — Independent creator Jashae Slaughter has launched apocalyptiverse.com — a browser-based platform that brings the characters and world of the Apocalyptiverse to life through collectible card packs, a live token economy, and original fiction, playable from any device at no cost to enter.

The platform centers on a card collection system tied directly to the Apocalyptiverse's published fiction. Characters from the books appear as collectible cards across common, rare, and mythic tiers — each linking back to the source material. Visitors earn Rivers Tokens through play and can spend them to pull card packs, creating a loop that connects readers to the wider universe without requiring a separate app or account outside the browser.

At launch, the platform features the Shadow of the Knight card set — the first batch of characters drawn from the series — alongside Cyber Citizen, a browser-based arcade game built around the Apocalyptiverse's sci-fi setting. A digital wallet system tracks each player's token balance and pull history, server-verified through a Cloudflare Worker backend.

The platform is free to access at apocalyptiverse.com. Physical books and comics from the series are available through Gumroad, Amazon, and GlobalComix.

About Apocalyptiverse

Apocalyptiverse is a transmedia sci-fi fiction universe spanning prose fiction, comics, collectible cards, and browser games, built around the fictional city of Rivers. The platform at apocalyptiverse.com functions as a gamified fiction economy, where story, card collecting, and gameplay are part of a single connected experience. Apocalyptiverse is created and published by Jashae Slaughter, also known as Xero for Hire.
