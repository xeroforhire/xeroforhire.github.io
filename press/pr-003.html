Cyber Citizen Launches on Apocalyptiverse.com

OMAHA, ARK. — May 1, 2026 — Apocalyptiverse has released Cyber Citizen, a browser-based moral choice game now live at apocalyptiverse.com. Set in the City of Rivers — the tech-noir urban world at the center of The Last Day series — the game puts players in the role of an ordinary citizen navigating the pressures and compromises of life in a dystopian city, two choices at a time.

Each session presents a series of scenarios drawn from life in the City of Rivers: street vendors, blackouts, suspicious packages, neighbors in need. Every decision has a consequence tracked across four citizen stats — morality, infamy, health, and addiction — building a profile that reflects the kind of citizen the player has chosen to become by the end of the run.

The game is designed as an entry point into the Apocalyptiverse for players who have not yet read the books. Rather than combat or collection, Cyber Citizen asks a simpler question: in a city where the system is working against you, what kind of person do you stay? The answer accumulates quietly across each choice until a final citizen rating is revealed.

Cyber Citizen is free to play in any browser at apocalyptiverse.com and is integrated with the platform's Rivers Token economy. The fiction it draws from — The Last Day series — is available through Gumroad, Amazon, and GlobalComix.

About Apocalyptiverse

Apocalyptiverse is a transmedia sci-fi fiction universe spanning prose fiction, comics, collectible cards, and browser games, built around the fictional city of Rivers. The platform at apocalyptiverse.com functions as a gamified fiction economy, where story, card collecting, and gameplay are part of a single connected experience. Apocalyptiverse is created and published by Jashae Slaughter, also known as Xero for Hire.
