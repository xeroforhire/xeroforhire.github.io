Battle Run Launches on Apocalyptiverse.com

OMAHA, ARK. — May 18, 2026 — Apocalyptiverse has released Battle Run, a browser-based card combat game now live at apocalyptiverse.com. The game marks a significant step in the evolution of the Apocalyptiverse platform — the point at which the fiction, the collectible card system, and the token economy stop being parallel tracks and start functioning as a single connected experience.

Where Cyber Citizen asked players to inhabit the world through choice, Battle Run asks them to fight through it using characters they actually own. Players select a hero card from their personal gallery and deploy it into three escalating rounds of street combat against the gangs, drones, and cyborgs of the City of Rivers. Card rarity — common, rare, or mythic — determines the hero's stats in the run. The stakes are real: a hero card lost in battle is burned from the player's collection permanently.

The combat system is built around a three-way type matchup — Brute, Tech, and Telekinesis — where every hero carries attacks across all three types, ensuring the player always has a viable option but never a guaranteed one. Enemies telegraph their attacks on a visible charge timer, giving players a window to react with a well-timed block or a type advantage. Rounds clear progressively, with a token-based healing opportunity between each, before a gang boss encounter closes the run.

Battle Run is the clearest expression yet of what Jashae Slaughter has been building toward since the platform launched in December 2025: a gamified sci-fi fiction economy where reading the books, collecting the cards, and playing the games are not separate activities but layers of a single universe — one where every asset has weight and every decision has consequence.

Battle Run is free to access at apocalyptiverse.com for any player with an active wallet and at least one card in their gallery. The fiction it draws from is available through Gumroad, Amazon, and GlobalComix.

About Apocalyptiverse

Apocalyptiverse is a transmedia sci-fi fiction universe spanning prose fiction, comics, collectible cards, and browser games, built around the fictional city of Rivers. The platform at apocalyptiverse.com functions as a gamified fiction economy, where story, card collecting, and gameplay are part of a single connected experience. Apocalyptiverse is created and published by Jashae Slaughter, also known as Xero for Hire.
