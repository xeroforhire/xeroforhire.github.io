City Brawlers Officially Launches with Exclusive Patriotic Characters Pack

OMAHA, ARK. — July 1, 2026 — Apocalyptiverse has officially launched The Last Day: City Brawlers and released the Patriotic Characters Pack — a limited card set featuring exclusive variants of Iron Knight, Alpha Red, and Zemara, available now through the platform's pack redemption system at apocalyptiverse.com.

City Brawlers is a browser-based tactical card combat game set in the City of Rivers, the tech-noir urban world at the center of The Last Day series. Players deploy hero cards from their own gallery into escalating dungeon encounters, spending Action Points, Energy, and Skill Points to chain attacks, build defenses, and outmaneuver enemies drawn from the fiction. Card rarity — common, rare, or mythic — determines a hero's stats in the run, and a card lost in battle is burned from the player's collection permanently.

The Patriotic Characters Pack (Pack 006–010) introduces exclusive flag-series art variants for three characters who represent different faces of strength within the Apocalyptiverse. Iron Knight, the tactical striker at the center of Shadow of the Knight, appears in four patriotic variants ranging from field portrait to full battlefield render. Alpha Red, whose story is currently in pre-order development, makes his card debut in five variants including a black-and-white sketch edition. Zemara rounds out the set with a single striking variant — her first appearance in the card system. All variants in this pack are exclusive to Batch 4 and will not be reissued.

The pack release marks the formal close of City Brawlers' beta period and the beginning of its integration into the full Apocalyptiverse economy — where collecting cards, earning Rivers Tokens, and playing games are connected activities rather than parallel ones. Future updates will expand the eligible hero roster and introduce new dungeon encounters drawn directly from upcoming releases in the series.

City Brawlers is free to play at apocalyptiverse.com for any player with an active wallet and at least one eligible hero card. The fiction behind the characters is available through Gumroad, Amazon, and GlobalComix.

About Apocalyptiverse

Apocalyptiverse is a transmedia sci-fi fiction universe spanning prose fiction, comics, collectible cards, and browser games, built around the fictional city of Rivers. The platform at apocalyptiverse.com functions as a gamified fiction economy, where story, card collecting, and gameplay are part of a single connected experience. Apocalyptiverse is created and published by Jashae Slaughter, also known as Xero for Hire.
