Apocalyptiverse Launches “Patriot Heroes” Card Set for America’s 250th Anniversary

OMAHA, ARK. — July 4, 2026 — 19 collectible cards, drawn from 16 original works by five independent creators, go live today — redeemable by code or directly through a new in-platform card shop.

Apocalyptiverse, the transmedia sci-fi fiction universe built around the city of Rivers, has launched its Patriot Heroes card set, a limited collection marking the 250th anniversary of the United States. The set reimagines core Apocalyptiverse characters — including Mars, Ghostwire, Iron Knight, Colonel Triumph, Breaker, and Eric the DREAD — in patriotic art, and introduces new characters contributed by independent creators from across the indie comics scene.

“This started as an open call to indie creators — bring your own characters, and we’d bring the house style,” said Jashae Slaughter, creator of Apocalyptiverse. “What came back was five different visions of what this country means to five different artists, and that’s exactly what a 250th anniversary set should look like.”

New Characters Joining the Apocalyptiverse Roster

  • Alpha Red, created by James Nguyen of Star Cross Comics
  • Atreydean Justice, created by Ralph Madrigal of Star Cross Comics
  • Zemara, created by Anthony Hunter of Silent Squirrel Comics
  • Starstruck, created by Joe Spicer of Star Cross Comics

Each contributed character was rendered in the same flag-and-monument visual treatment as the set’s returning heroes, unifying the collection under a single “America 250” identity while preserving each creator’s original character design.

A New Way to Collect

Alongside the set, Apocalyptiverse is rolling out a new feature on its Rivers City Terminal platform: a rotating card shop, where citizens can spend RVR tokens earned in-platform to buy directly into the Patriot Heroes set — no code required. Five cards are available at a time for 100 RVR each; the shop rotates automatically every 12 hours, or citizens can pay 50 RVR to reroll the selection early.

The set remains available the traditional way as well — through ten limited redeem codes distributed to the community, each unlocking a five-card pack from the full Patriot Heroes pool.

Availability

The Patriot Heroes set is live now at apocalyptiverse.com. Citizens can redeem a code, buy directly from the rotating shop, or browse the full collection on the Cards page.

About Apocalyptiverse

Apocalyptiverse is a transmedia sci-fi fiction universe spanning prose fiction, comics, collectible cards, and browser games, built around the fictional city of Rivers. The platform at apocalyptiverse.com functions as a gamified fiction economy, where story, card collecting, and gameplay are part of a single connected experience. Apocalyptiverse is created and published by Jashae Slaughter, also known as Xero for Hire.
