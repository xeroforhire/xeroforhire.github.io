The Last Day: Issue II Arrives August 4 — And Rivers City Is Already Open

OMAHA, ARK. — July 28, 2026 — Apocalyptiverse Comics today announced The Last Day: Issue II, the newest chapter of writer-artist Jashae Slaughter's transmedia science-fiction universe. The issue goes on sale Tuesday, August 4 on Gumroad, GlobalComix, and Nerdanatix, with print editions from Amazon and Gumroad to follow. Readers don't have to wait for the storefronts, though — Issue II is available to read right now inside the Reader Room at apocalyptiverse.com, a full week ahead of its retail debut.

Premise
In a future cyberpunk city, two friends explore an experimental VR machine — with unintended consequences for both of them.

Rendered entirely in black and white, The Last Day: Part 2 completes the two-part story Slaughter first launched with the series' 2024 debut. He describes finishing it as a turning point more than a finish line.

"I'm incredibly proud of this work and excited to be finishing it — but it's the greater story I'm most passionate about now that I've started building it," said Slaughter, who writes and illustrates the series under the name Xero for Hire.

From the Creator

My first real endeavor was adapting a story I'd built as an audio drama back in 2014 into a comic. I launched an Indiegogo campaign in 2023, reached the goal in 2024, and released the first issue that August. It was always a two-part story, and the second part ended up delayed a full year. I nearly gave up — but I pushed through, and now it's finished.

In adapting it, I took some creative liberties, focusing on the VR experience and what happens inside the sequence. I wanted to give context to Silas and his powers before sending them on a larger adventure.

Funding and making that first comic taught me how expensive and tedious the process is — and how much there was to learn. I kept studying indie comics and the craft of storytelling, and eventually moved into prose, writing several books that expanded the world before coming back to finish the second comic. I now have about five solid stories, two comics, and another series on the way at a much faster cadence.

That greater story is where my focus goes from here. With Part 2 out, I'll be building a new secondary series and finishing my seventh book — putting everything I've learned into a fun, expansive superhero universe.

About Jashae Slaughter

Jashae Slaughter, known online as Xeroforhire for more than 14 years, is a writer, podcaster, musician, and independent creator. He is the founder of the Apocalyptiverse press and creator of the sci-fi action thriller The Last Day, hosts The Xero for Hire Podcast, and records music as The Legend of XERO.
