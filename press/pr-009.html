Harbinger: Legend of the Seven Brothers Opens for Pre-Order Ahead of October 27 Release

OMAHA, ARK. — October 5, 2026 — Apocalyptiverse today opened pre-orders for Harbinger: Legend of the Seven Brothers, the fifth book in Jashae K Slaughter's The Last Day series, co-written with Calla Rae. The dark fantasy war epic releases October 27, 2026, and is available to pre-order now at apocalyptiverse.com/preorder.

Premise
Seven brothers. One endless war. Every name is feared, and none more than the youngest's.

Harbinger is set at -215 AW, centuries before the neon walls of Rivers, in an ancient China overrun by demon hordes. Seven brothers, bound to an emperor whose orders cannot be refused, become legends on the battlefield: the Titan, the Storm, the Dragon. But the soldiers who fight beside them fear one name above the rest. Mǎshì, the youngest, is the Harbinger, and the war is turning him into something even his brothers can't bear to watch.

A standalone prequel and mythic origin story, Harbinger requires no prior knowledge of the series. It contains battlefield violence throughout.

About Jashae K Slaughter

Jashae K Slaughter, known online as Xeroforhire for more than 14 years, is a writer, podcaster, musician, and independent creator. He is the founder of the Apocalyptiverse press and creator of the sci-fi action thriller The Last Day, hosts The Xero for Hire Podcast, and records music as The Legend of XERO.

About Calla Rae

Calla Rae is the co-author of Harbinger: Legend of the Seven Brothers, her collaboration with Jashae K Slaughter in the Apocalyptiverse.
