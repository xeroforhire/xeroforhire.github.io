Fiction published under the Apocalyptiverse, the independent press founded by Jashae Slaughter.

The fiction is published as The Last Day series under Xero For Hire Productions. Prose titles are co-written with Elias Blackwood.

Comics

The Last Day (Comic)
Comic · August 2024 · Issue #1

The 22-page launch issue that opened the Apocalyptiverse. Two friends, Steve and Silas, investigate a new full-dive VR arcade in their town — and when Silas is hooked into the machine, the game stops behaving like a game. Illustrated by Liilica Aoi, with prologue art by Studio Abiboge and cityscapes by Ailer Azusano. A Creator's Cut with sketches and behind-the-scenes lore is available on Gumroad.

Artist

The Legend of XERO
Christian Hip-Hop · 2012–present · Active

Jashae Slaughter's recording identity — a Christian hip-hop artist whose work blends rap, pop, and electronic/dance production, often built around speculative and superhero-tinged concepts. A Mid-Michigan recording artist and member of the Black Sheep Squad, with ties to Anno Domini Beats and the Christian rap community.
